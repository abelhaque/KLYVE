Questions

Everything merchants ask us.

What if the price of crypto crashes before I spend it?

You receive USDT — a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar. One USDT is always worth one dollar, so what you received is what you've got. Volatility is not your problem.

What does it cost my customer?

Nothing extra. They send the exact amount on the invoice, plus a network fee of around 1 USDT on the TRON network — typically less than a card fee they'd pay anyway.

Can a customer reverse the payment after delivery?

No. Crypto settlements are irreversible — there are no chargebacks. Once the funds arrive, the sale is final. This is the single biggest protection we offer merchants.

Do I need to understand crypto or pay tax in a special way?

No. Everything happens in one app with a normal transaction history. You receive USDT, spend it on your card like any balance, or convert to your bank. Keep the history for your accountant — it works just like a bank statement.

How do I know a payment has arrived?

Payments are detected on-chain within 1–2 seconds and credited to your balance instantly, with a notification. No more "pending" for days.

How long does setup take?

Minutes. Download the app, verify your identity, and you have a working payment address. Compare that to the weeks a merchant account application takes.
