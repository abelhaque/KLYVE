Questions

Everything people ask before their first tap.

Where can I actually spend it?

Anywhere Mastercard is accepted — over 100 million merchants in shops, online and in apps. The merchant never knows you paid with crypto; they see a normal card payment.

Will the value drop between topping up and spending?

No. Your balance is held in USDT, a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar. One USDT is always worth one dollar, unlike Bitcoin or other volatile coins.

How fast is "instant", really?

Incoming transfers are detected on-chain in about 1–2 seconds and credited to your balance immediately. Spending via Apple Pay or Google Pay is as fast as any bank card — because to the terminal, it is one.

What does it cost?

A simple 4% fee when funds arrive in your balance. Topping up, holding, and spending carry no extra charges beyond ordinary card or network costs.

Is it safe?

KYC-verified accounts, regulated card issuance through licensed partners, and stablecoins held with established custodians. Plus crypto payments to merchants are final — no one can claw back what you've sent.
